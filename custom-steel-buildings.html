What is a custom steel building?

A custom steel building is a fully engineered structure designed around your exact size, layout, and performance needs rather than pre-set dimensions.

Are custom steel buildings engineered for Florida's wind conditions?

Yes, our custom steel buildings are designed to meet Florida's wind load requirements and comply with local building codes, ensuring lasting durability.

Can I customize the size and layout?

Absolutely. You can customize dimensions, doors, windows, insulation, lean-tos, and structural features to match your project.

What does a custom steel building cost?

It depends on size, layout and options. The custom builds on this page came to $15,620 for a 28×45 carport with an enclosed lean-to, $22,792 for a 30×30 workshop with two lean-tos, and $42,775 for a 54×52 garage with a wrap-around lean-to. Prices are subject to change based on steel market conditions and local engineering requirements. Configure yours to see your number.
