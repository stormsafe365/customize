What Is Clear Span

Maximum Space, Zero Columns

A clear span steel building is a structure designed without interior load-bearing columns. The rigid-frame system transfers all weight directly to the outer walls, giving you an entirely uninterrupted interior.

This means every square foot of your building is usable — no columns to plan around, no obstructed sightlines, no wasted floor space.

  • Full-width open floor plans from wall to wall
  • Greater layout flexibility for any use case
  • Unrestricted equipment and vehicle movement
  • Maximized storage capacity
  • Easy future reconfiguration without structural limits

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "clear span" mean? ▾
Clear span refers to a building design without interior support columns. The rigid-frame structure transfers all loads to the exterior walls, providing a fully open interior space from wall to wall.
Are clear span buildings rated for hurricanes? ▾
Yes. Our certified clear span buildings are engineered to meet Florida's wind load requirements — rated for 170+ mph winds — and adhere to local building codes. Stamped engineering plans are included for permitting.
How wide can a clear span building be? ▾
Clear span widths vary depending on engineering requirements, but our buildings are designed to maximize open interior space without columns. Contact us with your specific width requirements and we'll engineer a solution.
How long does installation take? ▾
Most clear span steel buildings are installed in 1–5 days on site, depending on the size and complexity of the build. Pre-engineered framing significantly reduces construction time compared to traditional building methods.
Do I need a permit for a clear span building in Florida? ▾
In most Florida counties, yes — a building permit is required. Our certified clear span buildings come with stamped engineering drawings that meet Florida code, making the permitting process straightforward.
What warranty comes with a clear span building? ▾
All our clear span buildings include a 20-year rust-through warranty on the galvanized steel panels. Certified builds also include a lifetime structural warranty for added peace of mind.
